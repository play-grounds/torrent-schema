Enumeration · module bencode · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/BencodeType

The four value types of bencode. Keys of a dictionary are byte strings; the schema says which are UTF-8 text.

4 members

memberdescription
integer'i' <decimal digits, optional leading '-'> 'e'. No leading zeros (i03e is invalid), no negative zero (i-0e is invalid), no size limit: 64-bit is the practical floor for lengths.
byte string<decimal length> ':' <that many bytes>. Any bytes; the schema's valueType says whether a given string is UTF-8 text, raw bytes, or a concatenation of hashes.
list'l' <values> 'e'. Ordered; may be empty.
dictionary'd' <key value>* 'e'. Keys are byte strings and must appear in ascending order by raw bytes, each once. A decoder may accept unsorted keys for leniency, but an encoder never produces them.
