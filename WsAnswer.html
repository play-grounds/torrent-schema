Struct · module webtorrent · encoding json · 6 fields · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/WsAnswer

An answer to a forwarded offer. Sent by the answering peer with 'to_peer_id' naming the offerer; the tracker strips that and delivers it to the offerer as shown here, who matches 'offer_id' to the offer it made and completes the WebRTC connection. From then on the two peers speak the wire protocol over the data channel and the tracker is out of the picture.
