a real instance

what the tracker sent us 58 s after we announced for the snapshot with no offers of our own: the webtorrent 3 seeder's next announce carried offers, and tracker.openwebtorrent.com forwarded one, naming the seeder by peer_id (to_peer_id is not on it: that is the answer's field). Its SDP still says a=ice-options:ice2,trickle, which the rule ws-trickle flags — Node webtorrent does not strip it; browsers' clients do. Source: test/vectors/webtorrent/offer-forwarded.json.
