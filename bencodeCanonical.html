RuleSet · module bencode · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/bencodeCanonical

What makes a bencoding canonical. A strict decoder enforces these on input; every encoder satisfies them on output. encode(decode(x)) == x for any canonical x, which is what lets a torrent file be re-emitted byte-exactly and its infohash recomputed from its parts.
