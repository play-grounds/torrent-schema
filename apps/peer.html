A peer from the schema · Torrent Schema

A BitTorrent peer in this tab written from the schema and its codec, with no torrent library: it announces to a WebSocket tracker with a WebRTC offer (webtorrent), takes a seeder's answer, opens the data channel, exchanges handshakes (wire), reads the bitfield, requests one piece of a known torrent (the txbt4 UTXO snapshot by default) block by block and checks its SHA-1 against the torrent (metainfo). Two implementations that share no code, exchanging a verified piece.
