Enumeration · module tracker · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/AnnounceEvent

What an announce says about the peer's state. On the wire as a string over HTTP and a u32 over UDP.

4 members

codememberdescription
0noneA regular announce, repeated every 'interval' seconds. Over HTTP the 'event' parameter is absent or 'empty'.
1completedThe peer has just finished downloading. Sent once; counts in the tracker's 'downloaded' total.
2startedThe peer's first announce for this torrent.
3stoppedThe peer is leaving; the tracker drops it.

Used by AnnounceRequest, UdpAnnounceRequest, WsAnnounce.
