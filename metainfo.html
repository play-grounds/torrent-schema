Module · module metainfo · BEP 3 · layer metadata · v0.0.1 · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/metainfo

The .torrent file: a bencoded dictionary describing one file or one directory tree as a sequence of fixed-size pieces, each named by its SHA-1. The 'info' dictionary is the identity of a torrent: its SHA-1 over its canonical bencoding is the infohash, and everything outside 'info' (trackers, web seeds, comments, dates) can change without changing which torrent it is.
