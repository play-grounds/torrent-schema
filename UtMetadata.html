Struct · module wire · encoding bencode · 3 fields · BEP 9 · IRI https://torrent-schema.github.io/UtMetadata

Extension 'ut_metadata': the info dictionary fetched in 16 KiB pieces from a peer, so a magnet link suffices. A data message is this dictionary followed immediately by the piece's bytes; the whole metadata, once assembled, must hash to the infohash.
